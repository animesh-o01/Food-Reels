# 🍔 FoodReels — Discover Food Through Reels

FoodReels is a food discovery platform where users can explore delicious food through short, engaging reels uploaded by food partners (restaurant owners). Users can like and save their favorite food reels to discover dishes they love.

## ✨ Features

### 👤 User
- Browse and scroll through food reels.
- Like food reels you enjoy.
- Save your favorite reels for later.
- Discover dishes shared by different restaurants.

### 🍽️ Food Partner (Restaurant Owner)
- Register and log in as a food partner.
- Upload food reels showcasing their dishes.
- Share their restaurant's food offerings with users.
- Promote dishes through engaging short-form videos.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The goal of FoodReels is to make food discovery more interactive and engaging by combining short-form video content with restaurant promotion. It helps users discover new dishes while allowing restaurant owners to showcase their food visually.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** React.js
- **Backend:** Node.js, Express.js
- **Database:** MongoDB
- **Authentication:** JWT (if implemented)
- **API Testing:** Postman
